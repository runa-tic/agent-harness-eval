# Engineering handbook

How we build and run software here. Sections are numbered; changes made after
the last full revision are listed under Amendments at the end and take precedence over the section text.

## 1. Purpose of this handbook

The aim of purpose of this handbook is to make the next person's work easier, not to satisfy a checklist.
When purpose of this handbook is unclear, ask in the team channel before guessing.
This section on purpose of this handbook is reviewed yearly and changes are listed under Amendments.
The cost of poor purpose of this handbook is usually paid by the on-call engineer.
Teams may adapt the details of purpose of this handbook as long as the intent is kept and the adaptation is written down.
Most problems with purpose of this handbook come from skipping it under time pressure.
New joiners should see a worked example of purpose of this handbook in their first month.
Examples of purpose of this handbook done well are collected on the engineering wiki.
Do not automate purpose of this handbook before it has been done by hand enough times to understand it.
A short written summary of purpose of this handbook is better than a long meeting about it.
Nothing about purpose of this handbook replaces talking to the person affected.
Tooling for purpose of this handbook is owned by the Platform team; requests go through their board.
